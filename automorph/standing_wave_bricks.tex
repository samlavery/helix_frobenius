\begin{abstract}
Five bricks for certifier (a) of the rank-uniform registration program — the
derivation of each rung's standing wave from the carrier-lattice Poisson identity.  (1)~The $\mu_4$
conjugation clock of the dual trace channel: conjugation is frequency reversal, so
every even-rank dual term is real and every odd-rank dual term is purely imaginary, at
every frequency.  (2)~The arithmetic unfolding of the compiled rank-uniform lattice FE-pair:
the completed transform of the rank-$r$ matrix bank is
$\Lambda_r(s)=\pi^{-s}\Gamma(s)\sum_{n\ge1}r_{(r+1)^2}(n)\,n^{-s}$ on the half-plane —
the completed Epstein zeta of the carrier lattice, every rank, with its functional
equation already compiled.  (3)~The weighted strand transformation law: the degree-one harmonically
modulated strands exchange at shifted weight $3/2$ — the two-kernel, nontrivial-root
shape that even-rank rungs require, imported from the compiled odd Hurwitz pair.
(4)~\textbf{The standing wave of the lattice family}: the completed transform is real on
the central axis $\operatorname{Re}s=(r+1)^2/4$ at every rank — gate 2's exact shape,
derived from global Schwarz reality and the self-dual functional equation.  The
weak-side pipeline (geometric Poisson identity $\Rightarrow$ completed FE $\Rightarrow$ standing
wave) is thereby executed end-to-end on a genuine arithmetic family, uniformly in the
rank.  The remaining wall of certifier (a) is located exactly — the amplitude exchange,
one condition per rung, between two compiled endpoints — and is typed and
\emph{executed at} $\mathrm{GL}(1)$: the Dirichlet rung inhabits the exchange in
full, gate, continuation, standing wave, and functional equation all compiled.
\end{abstract}

\section{The $\mu_4$ conjugation clock}

The carrier-native dual channel at rank $r$
(\texttt{RankLadderParity.lean}) is
\[
I_r(\xi)=\int_{-1}^{1}\Phi(x)\,U_r(x)\,e^{-2\pi i x\xi c}\,dx,
\]
with $\Phi$ the even orbital profile and $U_r$ the Chebyshev Satake character.  The compiled parity clock
$I_r(-\xi)=(-1)^rI_r(\xi)$ now composes with the new conjugation clock
(\texttt{dualIntegral\_conj}: $\overline{I_r(\xi)}=I_r(-\xi)$, no hypotheses) into the
$\mu_4$ law $\overline{I_r(\xi)}=(-1)^r\,I_r(\xi)$: every even-rank dual
term is \emph{real} (\texttt{dualIntegral\_real\_even}) and every odd-rank dual
term is \emph{purely imaginary} (\texttt{dualIntegral\_imaginary\_odd}) — at every
rank, frequency, scale, and even profile.  The reality half of
the axis condition is compiled on the dual channel.

\section{The weighted strand}

The unweighted strand transforms to itself at weight $1/2$ with root number $1$.  The
degree-one harmonically modulated strands — the compiled odd Hurwitz pair — transform to
\emph{each other} at shifted weight $3/2$
(\texttt{WeightedStrandTransformation.lean}: \texttt{sinKernel\_inv},
\texttt{oddKernel\_inv}):
\[
\mathrm{sin}_a(1/t)=t^{3/2}\,\mathrm{odd}_a(t),
\qquad
\mathrm{odd}_a(1/t)=t^{3/2}\,\mathrm{sin}_a(t).
\]
Two different kernels, exchange closing only after two reflections, weight shifted by
the harmonic degree: the $d=1$ rung of the Hecke modulation ladder
$\widehat{P\cdot\mathrm{gauss}}=i^{-d}P\cdot\mathrm{gauss}$, and the exact geometric
shape of nontrivial root structure.  The $\Gamma_{\mathbb R}(s+1)$-completed Mellin
side and its functional equations are compiled upstream.

\section{The standing wave of the lattice family}

\begin{theorem}[the standing wave; machine-checked]
At every rank $r$, the completed transform of the matrix bank satisfies, on the
central axis $\operatorname{Re}s=(r+1)^2/4$:
\[
\Lambda_r(s)=\overline{\Lambda_r(s)}
\]
— gate 2's FE-on-axis shape with root number $1$; equivalently the transform is real
on the axis (\texttt{matrixLattice\_axis\_im\_zero}), and its entire part carries the
same standing wave.  The proof composes global Schwarz reality
$\Lambda_r(\bar s)=\overline{\Lambda_r(s)}$ (\texttt{matrixLattice\_schwarz}: the Mellin
transform of a real profile is Schwarz-real on the half-plane, globalized through the
entire part by the identity theorem) with the self-dual completed functional equation
(\texttt{matrixLattice\_FE}; the pair is its own reflection,
\texttt{matrixLatticePair\_symm\_eq}).  No new analytic input.
\end{theorem}

The weak-side pipeline of certifier (a) — geometric Poisson identity $\Rightarrow$ completed FE
$\Rightarrow$ standing wave — is thereby closed, end-to-end, on a genuine arithmetic
family, at every rank simultaneously.  Home: \texttt{LatticeStandingWave.lean}, theorem
\texttt{matrixLattice\_standingWave}, capstone \texttt{lattice\_weak\_rung}.

\section{Register}

\begin{remark}[register]
\textbf{Proved (machine-checked).}  All five bricks, as named above — including
the $\mathrm{GL}(1)$ exchange, whose only unconsumed datum is the nonvanishing of the
primitive root number (with primitivity of $\chi^{-1}$ carried as a hypothesis).

\textbf{The obstruction, measured.}  The rank-uniform reduction's cuspidal gate
(\texttt{TransferGate}) demands sub-axis primitive cancellation
$\|\sum_{k<n}a_k\|\le Cn^{\theta}$ with $\theta<\kappa/2$.  The lattice family cannot
supply it: its partial sums grow at the full abscissa — the two poles of
$\Lambda_r$, residues $\pm1$, are exactly the measured non-cancellation of the DC
mode.  The weak side is therefore the lattice family's home, and the standing wave above
is its gate-2 statement.

\textbf{The remaining wall, located.}  Certifier (a)'s outstanding input is the
\emph{amplitude exchange}: replace the representation-number amplitude by a rung
profile carrying the cancellation gate, while keeping the lattice transformation law.
One condition per rung, now positioned between two compiled endpoints — the geometric
Poisson identity with its standing wave on one side, the gate-and-reduction machinery
(\texttt{RankUniformReduction}) on the other.

\textbf{Cited, not formalized.}  The classical discharge of the exchanged rungs'
functional equations is symmetric-power automorphy: Kim--Shahidi at $r=3,4$
\cite{KimShahidi,Kim} and Newton--Thorne in general \cite{NT}; nothing here consumes
them.

\textbf{Not claimed.}  No rung's standing wave beyond the lattice family is claimed; the
dead coupling routes recorded in the program ledger (strand modulation, kernel
telescoping, Veronese folding, aligned bilateral summation) remain dead.

\textbf{Falsifier.}  A rank at which the lattice family's transform fails axis reality
would refute the standing wave and, through it, the compiled Schwarz or FE bricks; a
rung amplitude satisfying the cancellation gate whose completed readout fails the
reduction's FE would refute the gate capstone.
\end{remark}
