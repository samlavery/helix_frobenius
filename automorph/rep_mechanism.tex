\begin{abstract}
The transported banks of rung 7 --- one bank per finite representation datum
$\rho=\oplus_i\Sym^{r_i}$ of the level-one seed, with compiled summability and
$L$-factorization --- receive their analytic packages.  Two inputs make this uniform.
First, \emph{every} transported pair is self-dual: inverting every channel of a datum
is the blockwise clock reversal, so the contragredient coefficients equal the primal
coefficients at every $\rho$ --- one recursion, no per-datum argument.  Second, the
archimedean chart generalizes to the full Deligne form: an arbitrary finite list of
real clocks convolved in signed-log height onto the complex-pair kernel, with Mellin
multiplier exactly $\prod_j\Gr(s+\delta_j)\cdot\prod_i\Gm(s+\mu_i)$.  The mechanism
then holds at every datum and every chart type: one pointwise theta identity of the
prescribed $\rho$-readouts yields entirety, the functional equation, and the chart
identification for $L(\rho,s)=\prod_iL(\Sym^{r_i},s)$.  Named instances land the
remaining ladder banks at their classical charts: the degree-6 tensor
$\mathrm{std}\otimes\Sym^2$ at datum $[3,1]$, the degree-9 tensor
$\Sym^2\otimes\Sym^2$ at datum $[4,2,0]$, and split base change at datum $[1,1]$ ---
each from \emph{one} identity on its joint bank, with no per-factor input.
\end{abstract}

\section{Self-duality of every transported datum}

Rung 7's transport (\texttt{UniformTransport.lean}) assigns to every finite list
$\rho=[r_1,\dots,r_m]$ the channel type $\bigoplus_i\mathrm{Fin}(r_i{+}1)$, the
disjoint-union clock system, and the polynomial Satake pair
\texttt{repSatakePair}, whose bank is the Dirichlet product
$\prod_i c_{\Sym^{r_i}}$ (\texttt{repBank\_eq}).  The new input is that this pair is
its own contragredient at the coefficient level, for every datum:

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{RepMechanism.repPair\_dual\_eq\_primal}; machine-checked]
\label{thm:selfdual}
For every datum $\rho$ and every $j$, the contragredient radial Euler coefficients of
\texttt{repSatakePair} equal the primal ones.  The proof is one recursion: the channel
reversal $\sigma_\rho=\bigoplus_i\mathrm{rev}_{\,\mathrm{Fin}(r_i+1)}$
(\texttt{repPerm}) satisfies $w(x)^{-1}=w(\sigma_\rho x)$ on every channel
(\texttt{repWeight\_inv\_eq}: $\alpha^{-(r-2k)}=\alpha^{r-2(r-k)}$ blockwise), and
symmetric functions are permutation-invariant
(\texttt{radialGlobalSatakeCoeff\_comp\_equiv}).
\end{theorem}

Because the recursive channel type makes each step \emph{definitionally} a direct sum,
the induction consumes no reindexing: the case split is the case split of
\texttt{Sum.elim}.

\section{The general Deligne chart}

The complex-pair kernel ($\prod_i\Gm$) and the single-real mixed kernel
($\Gr\cdot\prod_i\Gm$) generalize by one list recursion
(\texttt{GlobalHelixGeneralChartCompletion.lean}):
\[
\texttt{generalKernelLog}\;[\,]\;\mu s=\texttt{completionKernelLog}\;\mu s,
\]
\[
\texttt{generalKernelLog}\;(\delta{::}\,\delta s)\;\mu s
=g_{\mathbb R}^{(\delta)}\ast_{\mathrm M}\texttt{generalKernelLog}\;\delta s\;\mu s,
\]
so the empty list is definitionally the complex kernel and a singleton the mixed
kernel --- both subsumptions are \emph{compiled}, at the kernel and at the 3D banks
(\texttt{generalKernelLog\_nil}, \texttt{generalKernelLog\_singleton}, and the
corresponding readout identifications, the definitional ones by \texttt{rfl}).
The three kernel facts hold at every real-shift list and every nonempty
complex-shift list: uniform boundedness at every admissible weight, decay faster than
every power, and the exact multiplier
\[
\texttt{generalKernelLog\_hasMellin}:\qquad
\widetilde g(s)=\prod_j\Gr(s+\delta_j)\cdot\prod_i\Gm(s+\mu_i)
\]
--- the general Deligne factor at the archimedean place.  The full 3D bank layer
(primal and reciprocal-height contragredient readouts, theta identifications, local
integrability, rapid decay, and both initial-half-plane identifications) is compiled
on this kernel, uniformly in the two shift lists.

\section{The mechanism at every datum and every chart}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{RepMechanism.mechanismG}; machine-checked]\label{thm:mechG}
For every self-dual-multiset bank, every real-shift list with nonnegative real parts,
every nonempty complex-shift list with nonnegative real parts, and every
$\varepsilon\neq0$: one pointwise theta identity between the prescribed general
readouts yields the general package (\texttt{GeneralNicePackage}) --- entire $\Lambda$,
$\Lambda(1-s)=\varepsilon\Lambda(s)$, and
$\Lambda(s)=\prod_j\Gr(s+\delta_j)\prod_i\Gm(s+\mu_i)\cdot L(s)$ on
$\operatorname{Re}s>B$ --- with uniqueness of the continuation
(\texttt{GeneralNicePackage.unique}), specialization to the complex and mixed packages
at empty and singleton real-shift lists (\texttt{toNice}, \texttt{toMixed}), and the
product law (\texttt{GeneralNicePackage.mul}: product bank, concatenated real and
complex shift lists, product root number).
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[the datum corollaries; machine-checked]\label{cor:rep}
\begin{center}
\texttt{rep\_package\_of\_theta},\quad \texttt{rep\_mixed\_package\_of\_theta},\\
\texttt{rep\_general\_package\_of\_theta}
\end{center}
For every representation datum $\rho$ of the seed and every admissible chart ---
complex, mixed, or general --- one theta identity of the prescribed $\rho$-readouts
yields the complete package for the compiled product bank
$L(\rho,s)=\prod_iL(\Sym^{r_i},s)$, at the uniform threshold of the datum.  The
self-duality input is Theorem~\ref{thm:selfdual}; nothing is assumed per datum.
\end{corollary}

\section{The remaining banks, at their classical charts}

Three named instances land the ladder's remaining banks.  In each, the entire
analytic superstructure of the \emph{joint} object comes from one identity on the
joint bank --- no per-factor package, no seed input:

\begin{corollary}[\texttt{tensor23\_package\_of\_theta}; machine-checked]
Datum $[3,1]$ --- the degree-6 tensor $\mathrm{std}\otimes\Sym^2$, whose bank is
$c_{\Sym^3}\!\star c_{\mathrm{std}}$ (the Clebsch--Gordan factorization compiled at
rung 4).  Chart: the pure-complex
$\Gm(s+\tfrac{k-1}2)\Gm(s+\tfrac{3(k-1)}2)\Gm(s+\tfrac{k-1}2)$ at weight $k=2n$ ---
the $\Sym^3$ pair and the seed shift.  One identity yields the package.  (The previous
finish of rung 4 consumed one identity \emph{and} the compiled seed rung; this
instance needs only the identity.)
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[\texttt{tensor33\_package\_of\_theta}; machine-checked]
Datum $[4,2,0]$ --- the degree-9 tensor $\Sym^2\otimes\Sym^2$, bank
$c_{\Sym^4}\star c_{\Sym^2}\star\zeta$ (rung 5's decomposition; the rank-zero
constituent is $\zeta$ by \texttt{symrBank\_zero\_eq\_zeta}, compiled).  Chart: the general
form with complex shifts pinned to the exponent pairs $k{-}1,\,2(k{-}1)$ (from
$\Sym^4$) and $k{-}1$ (from $\Sym^2$), and the real-shift list parametric with
nonnegative real parts --- classically three real clocks, one per even constituent
($\Sym^4$, $\Sym^2$, $\Sym^0$), with shifts fixed by the middle-line parities;
whichever admissible $(\delta s,\varepsilon)$ satisfies the identity yields the
package.  This is the first finish of the degree-9 object as one object: rung 5
end-to-end from a single equation.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[\texttt{bc\_split\_package\_of\_theta}; machine-checked]
Datum $[1,1]$, the split quadratic base-change bank
$c_{\mathrm{std}}\star c_{\mathrm{std}}$ --- rung 1's split factorization
\[
L(\mathrm{BC}\,\pi)=L(\pi)\,L(\pi\otimes\chi)\qquad(\chi\ \text{split}),
\]
at the doubled seed chart $\Gm(s+\tfrac{k-1}2)^2$.
\end{corollary}

\section{Register}

\begin{remark}[register]
\textbf{Proved (machine-checked).}  Everything named above: datum self-duality at
every $\rho$; the general kernel's bounds, decay, and multiplier; the general 3D bank
layer; the general mechanism with uniqueness; the datum corollaries at all three chart
types; the three named instances.  All at axiom footprint
$\{\texttt{propext},\texttt{Classical.choice},\texttt{Quot.sound}\}$, no
\texttt{sorry}.

\textbf{Consumed from the corpus.}  Rung 7's transport (channel recursion, bank,
summability), the mechanism engine of the companion note, the real and complex clocks,
and the theta/Mellin layer --- all compiled.

\textbf{Cited, not formalized.}  The classical discharges of the per-object identity:
Gelbart--Jacquet \cite{GJ} and Shimura \cite{Shimura} at $\Sym^2$, Kim--Shahidi
\cite{KimShahidi} and Kim \cite{Kim} at $\Sym^3,\Sym^4$ (hence at the tensor data
$[3,1]$, $[4,2,0]$), Langlands \cite{Langlands} at base change.  Named for scope, not
consumed.

\textbf{Not claimed.}  The truth of the theta identity for any datum beyond the seed
is not claimed; it is the isolated input, one pointwise equation per bank.  The
real-shift values of the degree-9 chart are not asserted; the theorem is uniform in
them.  Inert base change and automorphic induction keep their landed coefficient
identities (rungs 1--2) and are not datum instances: their twisted components are not
symmetric powers of the seed.

\textbf{Falsifier.}  A transported datum whose contragredient coefficients differ from
its primal coefficients at any index would refute Theorem~\ref{thm:selfdual}; a
general kernel whose Mellin multiplier deviates from $\prod\Gr\cdot\prod\Gm$ at an
admissible point, or a datum satisfying the identity whose $L$-readout fails the
package, would refute the mechanism.
\end{remark}
