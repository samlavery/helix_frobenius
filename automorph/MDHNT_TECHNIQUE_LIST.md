## A. LIFT AND HARMONIZATION

**A1. Harmonize on the object's own scale** — BUYS: a non-degenerate reading. HOW: read a
projected function and let its nature name its settings (scale/warp, adapters, clocks) so carrier +
fiber closes without residue. FAILS: unit-1 scale → **false null**. `universal.tex:469`. `discipline`

**A2. Carrier scale vs warp vs dwell — never blend** — carrier scale is a *constant*; warp is a
*function*; dwell re-registers values a projection skips. The reciprocal of a warp is a warp, so
**"inverse warp" does not exist**; removal is `WarpRemovalTransfer`. `universal.tex:469`. `proven`

**A3. Dress an arbitrary step into a harmonic one with a fiber spin** — BUYS: puts any native
spacing (log 7, √5, an irrational Satake angle) on a closing carrier without touching the object.
HOW: spin μ = π/m − H. FAILS: an n-dependent dressing is a warp and needs inverse-kernel removal.
`universal.tex:3453`. `proven`

**A4. Convert an irrational phase to a root-of-unity twist via Ω(n)** — HOW: pass to Θ(n) =
(π/3)Ω(n); the carrier phase becomes β^Ω(n) and the cell closes by Σβ^Ω(n) = o(X). FAILS: you need
a rate rather than o(X). `universal.tex:7217`. `borrowed` (classical inputs, novel assembly)

**A5. Admissibility = finite description complexity** — require v ↦ A_v(W) generated by a finite
rule fixed independently of how many places are queried. A source whose only faithful description
is its own sample path is **not admissible** — letting an adapter encode it would be oracular.
FAILS: omit this and every ∀∃ representation claim is trivially true. `discipline`

**A6. Constructive ∀ beats ∀∃** — state `∀W: Struct(W) ⟹ tuple = Synth(Struct(W))`, not `∀W ∃A_W`.
Adapter data are *read off* the finite law. Consequence: simultaneous synchronization is the
**common refinement** of individually synthesized systems — constructed, not posited.
`universal.tex:7418`. `discipline`

## C. REGISTRATION AND ALIGNMENT — the signature move

**C1. The registration law + the dichotomy** — `(chart) = (native) + (defect)`, defect
integer-valued, monotone, non-negative. One unabsorbed unit costs **linear** growth, so **sublinear

events. `proven, machine-checked`

**C2. What a real defect must look like** — integer, monotone, non-negative. *A sign-oscillating
count-minus-ramp is the **ledger**, not the defect.* Diagnostic: an event-free cell books exactly
−½. `erdos1095_proof.tex:218`. `discipline`

**C3. Registration gaps compose by a cocycle law** — `S_{H,L} = S_{H,K} + S_{K,L}`, so route a hard
gap through a convenient intermediate. **Sharpening: π/3 is not load-bearing** — the whole π/m
family registers identically. `universal.tex:3402`. `proven`

combinatorics. `L(t) = N(t) − θ(t)/π − 1`; `r_m = s_m + k − ½ − Στ_i`. **Free check:**
`r_m + Στ_i + ½ = s_{m+1} + 1` in every cell. The ½ is mean-of-drift, an artifact of the trapezoid.
`proven`

**C5. Subtract the continuous part to make jumps exact** — if S = N − const − (continuous clock)
then S and N have identical jumps, giving `Res_ρ(f′/f) = m_ρ` with **no simplicity hypothesis**.
`universal.tex:3520`. `proven`

**C6. Lattice rigidity — machine zero is zero** — the ℤ[ζ₆] norm form has smallest nonzero value 1,

**C7. Build the defect out of provably positive summands** — a global equality then forces per-term
vanishing; no adversary hides a contribution in cross-term cancellation. `universal.tex:2660`. `proven`

**C8. Identify at one sample, never at the trace** — prove pointwise kernel equality first; sum
only after basiswise equality. Removes "maybe two errors cancelled". `universal.tex:3055`. `proven`

**C9. Hermitian/anti-Hermitian split** — *compute* `P_nd(H) = ½(H+H†)`, don't postulate; the global
claim becomes a fixed-point equation holding **basiswise**. `universal.tex:2991`. `proven`

**C10. Path-lift through exp to kill branch choices** — show the path avoids 0, lift through exp
with a real initial value; path-lift uniqueness removes every branch choice and the winding integer
is an **output**. `universal.tex:2538`. `borrowed` (standard rigorous S(t))

**C11. Derive the normalization constant from geometry, not convention** — the `+1` in

**C12. Reachability once, not genericity per cell** — two ℝ-independent directions span ℂ, so any

**C13. Coherent generator suite — the resisting cell names its adapter** — corrections drawn from

**C14. Pre-registered cell scale + anti-aliasing law** — draw the modulus M from the root-of-unity

## E. ANALYTIC CRAFT

**E1. The exact gauge — move to the coordinate where the triangle inequality is lossless** —
represent `A(x) = (1/2πi)∫M(u)x^{−u/2}du` so the integrand is *real magnitude × deterministic
phase*; then `|A(x)| ≤ B_τ x^{−τ/2}` with B_τ **independent of x**. Replaces a ~60-page oscillation
estimate. FAILS: the phase is not deterministic in that coordinate. `universal.tex:2085`.
`proven` / `borrowed` (Watson–Mellin + non-stationary phase)

**E2. The x-free magnitude bound** — parameters enter **only** through a single invariant scale, so
all parameter dependence leaves as a pure power. *The technique is "find the single invariant
scale."* `measured + certified`

**E3. Evaluate the oscillatory integral in closed form instead of estimating it** — Mellin
representation, Fubini, Taylor at the endpoint, each monomial an exact Γ. `universal.tex:8038`. `proven`

**E4. Free contour height = free exponent** — take the remainder's contour at any τ₁ > 0 for
arbitrary polynomial saving at no cost. `universal.tex:8070`. `proven`

**E5. Difference against a fixed base point to make a divergent trace converge** — the Hadamard
counterterm. `universal.tex:3628`. `borrowed`

**E6. Split the Mellin at 1 and reflect the lower half** — entirety with poles isolated as an
explicit finite list. `universal.tex:5818`. `borrowed` (**this is Hecke's converse-theorem proof**)

**E7. Genuine strip boundedness from the reflected integral** — `|C(σ+it)| ≤ ∫₁^∞|K|(x^b + x^{κ−a})dx/x`,
t-independent. No Phragmén–Lindelöf. **Two traps flagged:** "bounded in vertical strips" ≠

**E8. Bounded primitive ⟹ continuation past the abscissa** — from a purely combinatorial
cancellation statement, consuming no FE and no automorphy. `borrowed` (Landau)

**E9. The grown primitive — replace the clip and the barrier disappears** — use
`A^e(x) = Σλ_n e^{−n/x}` (continuous entry, no edge); then `∫A^e x^{−s−1}dx = Γ(s)L(s)`. Measured
exponents drop 0.268 → 0.002. FAILS: **the random control keeps θ = 0.581** — smoothing manufactures

`borrowed` (Riemann's own smoothing); the *diagnostic use* is the contribution

**E10. Mellin-pole calibration** — shift to Re s = −½ crossing only the Γ-pole at s = 0: automorphy

**E11. Reduce a chain to one measurable scalar gate** — `θ_W := inf{θ : A_W(x) ≪ x^θ} < κ/2`
places the axis inside the analyticity strip. No warp, no automorphy, no FE, no Poisson consumed.

**E12. Axis degeneration — prove where the hard leg collapses, then propagate** — on Re s = κ/2,

**E13. Compose the completion kernel out of exact pairwise units** — the Mellin convolution of two

**E14. AM–GM saddle to kill a theta tail** — a two-line inequality splits a coupled two-term

**E15. Normalize before peeling, so the poles align** — peeling from the un-normalized Rankin square

## F. TOWERS AND FUNCTORIALITY

**F1. Towers — climb with one ruler** — rank-uniform instruments; *rank-uniformity is where limits
come from*. If ρ^r ≤ C for **every** rung then ρ ≤ 1; with the inverse, ρ = 1 exactly — that **is**
temperedness, and on the carrier: the strand is a **helix, not a spiral**. `proven`

**F2. When composition misses degrees, use the additive tensor route** — plethysm has multiplicative
top weight ab and **misses the prime degrees**; Clebsch–Gordan gives `π ⊗ Sym^{r−1} = Sym^r ⊕
Sym^{r−2}`, so every rank is the top block. No degree wall. `universal.tex:6437`. `proven`

**F3. Restate the theorem at the minimal data it actually reads** — list what the argument consumes,
restate there, then **verify on a system outside the original family**. The outside-family test is
what catches a silently used hypothesis. `universal.tex:7063`. `proven + measured`

**F4. Reduce invariance to generators by torus conjugation** — `D(q)T(1)D(q⁻¹) = T(q)`, so diagonal
invariance plus one unit winding per direction forces full GL(n,K). Makes a rational-parameter
density step unnecessary. `borrowed`

**F5. Vandermonde faithfulness of a moment tower** — on distinct frequencies every nonzero amplitude
state is detected at finite depth. FAILS: coincident frequencies; **and it says nothing about
cycles**, which contribute *extension* data, not a semisimple amplitude. `borrowed` (Prony)

**F6. The effective ledger — collapse the infinite tower quantifier to bank size, then prove it
sharp** — a nonzero state on m clocks fires within the first m readings, and every bank carries a
**maximally hidden** state silent through the first m−1. `hodge_companion.tex:623`. `proven`

**F7. First nonzero depth localizes the structure** — scan the tower and record the 0,…,0,≠0
signature. Every concrete class tested fires at a finite first depth (Tate/MW/Sha at 1, CM at 2,
Ceresa at 3); none silent-forever. `measured`

**F8. Drift = the Jordan shear a clock stack cannot produce** — value-blind (every trace power is
δ-independent), jet-visible (the shear slot of the d-th power is `d·λ^{d−1}·δ` — the Gross–Zagier
shape), never silent. On the model, Beilinson–Bloch anisotropy is a **Vandermonde fact**. `proven`

**F9. Identity and composition are the decisive functoriality tests** — verify `W_id = id` and
`W_{r∘s} = W_r∘W_s` at weight-system level with each composite an exact disjoint union. **Then

## H. VERIFICATION AND CONTROLS

**H1. Precision-tracking as the identity test** — an identity's residual **tracks precision**
(1e−29/1e−48/1e−79 at 30/50/80 digits); a model floor is digit-independent. Report the sweep, not a
number. *Most portable single move in the corpus.* `measured`

**H2. Ablate every stage** — run the full stack and each stage removed. Every ablation floors at
O(1)–O(100) while the full stack tracks precision. Load-bearing **measured, not argued**. `measured`

**H3. The arithmetic-neutrality audit** — run the instrument on {true, detuned, random} fibers of
the same structural class. Identical behaviour ⟹ **arithmetic-neutral** ⟹ cannot be evidence for an
arithmetic conclusion. *This is what demoted forcible cell closure to "the transport half."*
`measured + discipline`

**H4. Compile the non-discrimination theorem for your own device** — prove one stage closes any two
residuals identically, so forcibility carries **no information about the fiber** and can never serve
as niceness evidence (`forcible_nondiscriminating`). `universal.tex:6410`. `proven` — *the most
honest instrument in the corpus*

**H5. Two controls that fail on opposite gates** — one passing A failing B, one the reverse. The
gates are separated by dedicated counterexample classes, not by argument. `measured`

**H6. Same-term-algebra control** — feed the identity a system with the *identical per-term algebra*
but different coefficients. Target closes at 2e−16, control fails by 1.1e2 ⟹ coefficient-sensitive,

138–149; Diaconis–Fulman AMM 116 (2009) 788–803); eigenvalues 1, p⁻¹, …, and the stationary
distribution is **Eulerian, independent of p**. What the house version adds is the conditioning on a
prescribed sum.

**L3. The 𝔽₂ valuation-parity ledger** — turns "product of factorials is a square" into linear
algebra: `w(a) = w(a−1) + u(a)` with u multiplicative and w its cumulative sum, so pairing
consecutive arguments **annihilates the cumulative part**. Existence becomes span-membership.
**Fresh-coordinate obstruction:** a prime turns on its own coordinate first, so it is independent of
all predecessors. `proven`

**L4. The LP / fractional-knapsack ceiling** — payoff 1/p, cost β_p; the ratio is **strictly
decreasing**, which is exactly the exchange lemma's hypothesis, so the optimum is a **prefix**.
FAILS **five ways, all of which have bitten**: the ratio is non-monotone at q ≥ 3; the search cap
silently changes the answer; degenerate rails must be excluded (**tell: the spurious optimum has
Σβ = 1 exactly and *is attained***); the supremum is not attained; a monotone family's extremum is
not rigidity. `proven given the budget rule` — **the budget rule is Bloom–Croot Conjecture 1**

**L5. When is a restatement informative rather than tautological — three tests** — (T1) **disjoint
machinery** (proved by something independent of the target's definition); (T2) **a strictly finer
exact companion**, so you can *see the slack*; (T3) **operational payoff** — and the wrong-modulus
failure proves content: *naming the right modulus IS the content.* `discipline`

**L6. Saturated digits are rigid** — where k has a base-p digit p−1, domination forces **equality**,
collapsing an inequality to a congruence. `L(k)` = the *k-smooth part of k+1* = k+1 if composite, 1
if prime. Cuts the search by L(k). FAILS: substituting lcm(1..k) makes it false at k = 3. `proven`

**L7. Exact CRT density, and its legitimacy condition** — independence is **exact** iff each rail's
condition lives on a **bounded** modulus and the moduli are coprime. The moment a rail's condition
involves *all* digits, the product is a **model** and asserting it *is* the transversality assumption
you were trying to prove. **That is the wall, not a technicality.** `proven` / `discipline`

**L8. Swap the variable — a pseudorandom condition on n is a deterministic interval union in p** —
fix n, vary p: nothing probabilistic enters and Mertens **evaluates** the mass. FAILS when the
intervals fall below unit length — *an earlier draft summed Mertens over intervals shorter than 1 and
was withdrawn.* `proven on the stated range`

**L9. The antipodal law** — for p above a threshold the whole ledger is one residue, and the divisor
arc and carry arc are **complementary**: a negative shift forces extinction, a positive shift is
automatic. `borrowed` — **Pomerance, AMM 122 (2015), §5**, both arcs, same threshold

**L10. The a-fold extinction tower** — `p^a | n−i` with `p^{a+1} > qn` kills **every** level, so the
window must be smooth *and* its square-full part smoother *and* so on. Kills the natural parametric
families uniformly. `proven` (the a ≥ 2 cascade is a one-line extension of Pomerance's a = 1)

**L11. The arc fan** — the single window lemma becomes a fan of q−1 arcs, the j-th of length k/j at
height n/j. `proven`

**L12. The Boolean-ledger bound** — `v_p ≤ ⌈log₂ q⌉` for p > n/q, **sharp**. So the binomial ledger
is a single bit iff q ≤ 2 and a staircase above. `proven`

**L13. Dispose of most of the range, and quote the proportion *with its range*** — 99.38% over
[10,3000) but 99.707% over [2001,6000). **No single percentage characterises a reduction.** `proven`

**L14. Identification with a named problem + the conjunction test** — the imported literature must
supply the **conjunction** you need, not one conjunct. `proven`

**L15. The rate is the position of the wall, not a property of the estimates** — if your rate equals
the trivial bound on the range you fail to cover, sharpening estimates **cannot** help; only
extending the range can. `proven`

**L16. When the main term is < 1, the error IS the answer** — budget and second-moment arguments are
structurally blind to a set of size three. **Check main-term size before choosing a method.** `measured`

**L17. Self-bootstrapping an implicit inequality** — `G ≤ C + log₂(G+1)` plus a trivial a priori
bound gives the result in one step, killing a citation. **Before citing a bound for an error term,
check whether the inequality bootstraps against itself.** `proven`

**L18. Tuple-valued vs point-valued objectives** — extension moves (append-a-unit) exist only for
the former. A cheap first classification for any new problem. `proven`

**L19. Classify the target before attacking** — *uniform-in-n* (hardest, intersection/transversality)
→ *positive-probability* (softer, density) → *fluctuation* (softest, computationally accessible).
`discipline`

**L20. THE LADDER — prove where every rail cooperates, then transfer** — the prototype is Spiro's
τ(n) = τ(n+5040) (5040 = 7!, smooth, divisible by every small rail), with Heath-Brown then removing
the crutch. The ledger's job is to **locate the cooperative parameter**. FAILS: prove the cooperative
case and call it progress — **the transfer step is the theorem**. **ORPHAN: never run, on any
problem.** `discipline` / `orphan`

**L21. Maier–Tenenbaum's log 3 − 1 is a per-rail state count** — three states per rail ⟹ 3^k
against range (log n)^1. **Identical in form to β_p and to the knapsack budget.** The consequence is
the technique: *Erdős–Hall did the counting side, Maier–Tenenbaum the achievement side — and the
achievement side is always ours to find.* `borrowed`

**L22. Almost-all → all is THE boundary** — whenever the ledger gives an easy almost-all result, the
all-n version is likely a named open problem. **Check before claiming a route.** `discipline`

**L23. Is the object secretly a known function?** — sandwich it between known functions before
assuming difficulty. This dissolved a conjecture as **mis-posed, not hard**. Run it first; it is
cheap. `discipline`

**L24. One paper is load-bearing across a cluster** — identify the cluster's engine once.
*"Ledger locates, Ψ(x,y) closes."* `discipline`

**L25. Reduction-to-sparse-residual, with claim calibration** — the honest exchange rate: **a
completed reduction is roughly one line of progress**, because the residual is the paper. `discipline`

## M. THE STANDING DIAGNOSIS

The ledger gives everything **multiplicative or per-rail**. Every problem's actual question needs a
**collision or density** statement, which the ledger does not reach. Across a nine-problem Erdős
campaign it closed **none** of them. The missing instrument is **L20, the ladder**, and it has never
been run.
