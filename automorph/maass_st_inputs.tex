\begin{abstract}
Let $\pi$ be a Hecke--Maass cusp form for $\mathrm{SL}_2(\Z)$ with Satake
parameters $\alpha_p$ and angles $\theta_p$.  The Sato--Tate conjecture for
$\pi$ --- temperedness at every prime together with equidistribution of
$\{\theta_p\}$ for the semicircle measure --- remains open; unlike the
holomorphic case it is untouched by symmetric-power modularity of
Newton--Thorne, whose method does not reach Maass forms.  This note records,
with no conditional statement anywhere except where a hypothesis is explicitly
named as such: (1) an \emph{exact} two-input reduction of the conjecture, each
input equivalent to the conclusion it feeds, so that the interface provably has
zero slack; (2) the unconditional moment record --- the first nine moments of
$\{\theta_p\}$ converge to the Sato--Tate moments (Kim--Shahidi), with the
complete tensor-tower coefficient algebra behind the record machine-checked,
and the local Rankin--Selberg factor exhibited as an exact sum of squares;
(3) an extension of the reachable \emph{joint} moment matrix for a pair of
Maass forms by the three entries $(5,1)$, $(6,1)$, $(5,2)$, via automorphic
vehicle identities, stated at their precise analytic grade; (4) two structural
insufficiency theorems delimiting what any future proof must contain: no
Satake-independent analytic payload can supply converse-theorem inputs, and
every factorization of the missing $\GL(4)$-twist family for $\Sym^5$ through
the monoid of known automorphic objects requires one of exactly two unknown
functorial products; and (5) the frontier stated exactly: $\Sym^5$ automorphy
--- the gateway past the record --- has two equivalent faces, one new
functorial product or a global converse theorem at half twist range together
with converse-grade entirety, and beyond finitely many rungs any route to the
full conjecture requires an engine not present in current mathematics.
\end{abstract}

\section{The conjecture and the exact reduction}\label{sec:reduction}

Fix a Hecke--Maass cusp form $\pi$ for $\mathrm{SL}_2(\Z)$, unramified
everywhere, with unitarized Satake parameters
$\{\alpha_p,\alpha_p^{-1}\}$ and $S_r(\alpha)=\sum_{j=0}^r\alpha^{r-2j}$ the
symmetric-power trace ladder.  The Sato--Tate conjecture asserts
(i) $|\alpha_p|=1$ for every $p$, and (ii) equidistribution of the angles
$\theta_p$ with respect to $\tfrac{2}{\pi}\sin^2\theta\,d\theta$.

\begin{theorem}[exact reduction; machine-checked]\label{thm:reduction}
Let $M$ be the seed datum of $\pi$.  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a per-rung polynomial ceiling
$\|\alpha_p^{\pm r}\|\le C_p\,(r+1)^k$ for all $r\ge 1$ exists \emph{if and
only if} $\pi$ is tempered at every prime;
\item given temperedness, the vanishing of the prime averages of
$S_r(\alpha_p)$ for every $r\ge1$ is \emph{equivalent} to Sato--Tate
equidistribution of the angles.
\end{enumerate}
Consequently any proof of the conjecture must produce these two inputs at
full strength: the interface has zero slack.
\end{theorem}

The two equivalences are the compiled statements
\texttt{temperedness\_iff\_polyCeiling} and
\texttt{cancellation\_iff\_equidistribution}, assembled in
\texttt{maass\_satoTate\_exact} (file \texttt{MaassSatoTateClosure}); the
polynomial-to-uniform ceiling manufacture is
\texttt{MaassPolyCeiling.toCeiling}.  Serre's criterion \cite{Serre} converts
input (2) into the one-line theory of $L(\Sym^r\pi,s)$: holomorphy and
nonvanishing on $\Re s\ge 1$, for every $r$.

\section{The unconditional moment record}\label{sec:record}

\begin{theorem}[Kim--Shahidi; Gelbart--Jacquet, Kim]\label{thm:record}
For $\pi$ as above and generic (non-dihedral) type:
$\Sym^r\pi$ is automorphic for $r\le 4$
\cite{GJ,KimShahidiAnnals,KimJAMS}; $L(\Sym^r\pi,s)$ admits meromorphic
continuation with functional equation for $r\le 9$, holomorphic and
nonvanishing on $\Re s\ge 1$ in the range needed by Serre's criterion
\cite{KimShahidiDuke,KimShahidiIMRN}.  Hence the first nine moments of
$\{\theta_p\}$ converge to the Sato--Tate moments.
\end{theorem}

Three layers behind this record are machine-checked in the corpus:

\begin{proposition}[tensor-tower coefficient algebra; machine-checked]
\label{prop:tower}
For a Hecke eigen-datum with Satake $\alpha$, the coefficient banks satisfy,
as exact identities of arithmetic functions at every $n$:
$\mathrm{std}\otimes\Sym^2=\Sym^3\oplus\mathrm{std}$,
$\mathrm{std}\otimes\Sym^4=\Sym^5\oplus\Sym^3$,
$\Sym^2\otimes\Sym^4=\Sym^6\oplus\Sym^4\oplus\Sym^2$,
$\Sym^3\otimes\Sym^4=\Sym^7\oplus\Sym^5\oplus\Sym^3\oplus\mathrm{std}$,
$\Sym^4\otimes\Sym^4=\Sym^8\oplus\Sym^6\oplus\Sym^4\oplus\Sym^2\oplus 1$,
together with their $L$-series forms on the common half-plane of absolute
convergence.
\end{proposition}

(Files \texttt{TensorTowerBanks}, declarations \texttt{tensorCube\_bank}
through \texttt{tensorSym4Sym4\_bank} and the \texttt{\_LSeries} forms;
the trace-level engine is the Clebsch--Gordan law
$S_k S_m=\sum_{j\le m}S_{k+m-2j}$, \texttt{clebsch\_gordan\_trace} in
\texttt{RSLocalCauchy}.)

\begin{proposition}[local positivity, exact; machine-checked]
\label{prop:sumsq}
Let $rs_k$ denote the $k$-th coefficient of the local Rankin--Selberg factor
$\bigl((1-\alpha^2x)(1-x)^2(1-\alpha^{-2}x)\bigr)^{-1}$.  Then
$rs_{k+2}-rs_k=S_{k+2}(\alpha)^2$ with $rs_0=S_0^2$, $rs_1=S_1^2$; hence
$rs_k=\sum_{j\le k/2}S_{k-2j}(\alpha)^2$, and at a unit strand every $rs_k$
is a nonnegative real --- positivity is an identity, not an estimate.
\end{proposition}

(\texttt{rs\_local\_cauchy}, \texttt{rsCoeff\_eq\_sum\_sq},
\texttt{rs\_local\_positivity}.)  The record stops at nine because the
Langlands--Shahidi list of Eisenstein configurations is exhausted at $E_8$
\cite{KimShahidiIMRN}; no Eisenstein series in existence reaches $r\ge 10$.

\section{Twisted families, grade, and the joint moment matrix}
\label{sec:joint}

The following automorphic \emph{vehicle} identities carry analytic control
past the naive pair list.  All decompositions below are machine-checked as
exact identities of coefficient banks; the analytic conclusions use the cited
classical inputs: automorphy of the Kim--Shahidi product $\sigma_2\boxtimes
\sigma_3$ in both roles \cite{KimShahidiAnnals}, of $\Sym^{\le4}$
\cite{GJ,KimShahidiAnnals,KimJAMS}, Rankin--Selberg theory for automorphic
pairs \cite{JPSS}, and nonvanishing on $\Re s=1$ \cite{ShahidiNonvanishing}.

\begin{theorem}[twisted families at Serre grade]\label{thm:twisted}
For every $\GL(2)$ cusp form $g$ and every $\GL(3)$ cusp form $\tau$:
\[
L\bigl((g\boxtimes\Sym^2\pi)\times\Sym^3\pi,s\bigr)
 = L(g\times\Sym^5\pi,s)\,L(g\times\Sym^3\pi,s)\,L(g\times\pi,s),
\]
\[
L\bigl((g\boxtimes\Sym^2\pi)\times\Sym^4\pi,s\bigr)
 = L(g\times\Sym^6\pi,s)\,L(g\times\Sym^4\pi,s)\,L(g\times\Sym^2\pi,s),
\]
\[
L\bigl(\Sym^4\pi\times(\pi\boxtimes\tau),s\bigr)
 = L(\Sym^5\pi\times\tau,s)\,L(\Sym^3\pi\times\tau,s).
\]
In each identity the left side and all right-hand factors except one are
Rankin--Selberg $L$-functions of pairs of automorphic representations; hence
$L(g\times\Sym^5\pi,s)$, $L(g\times\Sym^6\pi,s)$ and
$L(\Sym^5\pi\times\tau,s)$ are holomorphic and nonvanishing on
$\Re s\ge 1$.
\end{theorem}

(Bank identities: \texttt{quinticG\_bank}, \texttt{vehicle\_bank},
\texttt{quinticTau\_bank} --- the last universal over an arbitrary abstract
$\GL(3)$ Satake family, so no automorphy enters the compiled layer.)

\begin{proposition}[grade dichotomy]\label{prop:grade}
The division chains of Theorem~\ref{thm:twisted} yield holomorphy and
nonvanishing on the closed half-plane $\Re s\ge1$ (\emph{Serre grade}) and
cannot yield entirety on $\C$ (\emph{converse grade}): a quotient inherits
poles at the interior zeros of its denominator, and no unconditional
zero-free region covers the critical strip.  Converse-theorem applications
therefore require entirety inputs of the Kim--Shahidi type
\cite{KimShahidiEntire}, which division does not supply.
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}[joint moment matrix for a Maass pair]\label{cor:joint}
Let $\pi,\pi'$ be Hecke--Maass cusp forms, not twist-equivalent.  The mixed
moments $\tfrac{1}{\pi(N)}\sum_{p\le N}S_a(\theta_p(\pi))S_b(\theta_p(\pi'))$
converge to $0$ for all $(a,b)$ with $a,b\le 4$, for $(a,b)=(r,0)$ with
$r\le 9$, and for
\[
(a,b)\in\{(5,1),\ (6,1),\ (5,2)\},
\]
the last three via Theorem~\ref{thm:twisted}.
\end{corollary}

The compiled layer identifies the joint-moment averages with the prime
averages of the corresponding banks: the degree-one local Euler coefficient
is the sum of the local weights (\texttt{radialLocalEulerCoeff\_one}), hence
any bank's value at a prime is its local weight sum
(\texttt{bank\_at\_prime}), hence the three banks read
$S_5S_1$, $S_6S_1$, $S_5S_2$ at every prime
(\texttt{joint\_51\_at\_prime} etc., file \texttt{JointMoments}).

\begin{remark}[record check]\label{rem:check}
Whether the entries $(5,1),(6,1),(5,2)$ --- and the $\GL(3)$-twisted family
of Theorem~\ref{thm:twisted} --- appear in the existing joint-Sato--Tate
literature is an open bibliographic obligation of this note; a targeted
search of the Kim--Shahidi applications papers and recent joint-distribution
work did not surface them, and no novelty is claimed pending a full reading.
\end{remark}

\section{Structural insufficiency theorems}\label{sec:insufficiency}

Any future proof of the conjecture must contain content of a specific shape.
Two theorems delimit it.

\begin{theorem}[Satake-blind payloads are insufficient]\label{thm:blind}
No family of completed analytic objects that is independent of the Satake
data can supply the twisted inputs of any converse theorem.  In particular:
the carrier completion attached to the polynomial Satake class satisfies
identical functional equations for distinct data and distinct twists
(machine-checked: the completed transform of the radius-live niceness class
is literally the same function for different base pairs and twists), while
the one-sided converse inputs distinguish real objects --- the unit (zeta)
datum inhabits the class and its one-sided reflection is refuted
(\texttt{zeta\_no\_package}, \texttt{zeta\_reflection\_fails}), and the
maximally non-tempered spiral datum inhabits the radius-live class while a
$\GL(2)$ automorphic representation with its parameters would contradict
Rankin--Selberg bounds.  A discrimination-free source cannot feed a
discrimination device.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[the monoid gap at $m=4$]\label{thm:monoid}
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the monoid of automorphic objects generated from
$\GL(2)$ cusp forms by $\Sym^{\le4}$, the products
$\GL(2)\boxtimes\GL(2)$ \cite{Ramakrishnan} and $\GL(2)\boxtimes\GL(3)$
\cite{KimShahidiAnnals}, isobaric sums, duals and character twists.  Then for
a $\GL(4)$ cusp form $\tau_4$, every factorization of
$\tau_4\otimes\Sym^5\pi$ as $A\otimes B$ with $A,B\in\mathcal{A}$ requires an
automorphic product on $\GL(2)\times\GL(4)$ or $\GL(3)\times\GL(3)$ ---
neither of which exists \cite{Cogdell}.  The verification is finite
bookkeeping over the rank splits, including the self-dual sub-families
$\tau_4=g\boxtimes h$ and $\tau_4=\Sym^3 h$.
\end{theorem}

Together with the exhaustion of the Langlands--Shahidi list at $E_8$ and the
failure of Euler structure for four or more copies of a $\GL(2)$ form against
Siegel--Eisenstein pullbacks (the trilinear invariant functional is unique;
from four factors on, invariant functionals smear into spectral integrals
whose inversion presupposes the sought continuation), these results close
every known route to rung ten and beyond.

\section{The frontier, exactly}\label{sec:frontier}

\begin{proposition}[two faces of the gateway]\label{prop:faces}
$\Sym^5\pi$ automorphy follows from either:
\emph{(A)} niceness of the twist family $L(\tau_4\times\Sym^5\pi,s)$ at
converse grade --- by Theorem~\ref{thm:monoid} requiring one new functorial
product; or
\emph{(B)} the global converse theorem for $\GL(6)$ with twists up to
$\GL(3)$ (the global Jacquet conjecture at $n=6$, open; its local analogue is
a theorem \cite{Chai,JacquetLiu}) \emph{together with} converse-grade
entirety of the $m\le3$ family, which by Proposition~\ref{prop:grade} the
present division chains do not supply.
\end{proposition}

Even both faces falling extends the tower only finitely (to $\Sym^6$, with
the compiled cascade identity
$(\mathrm{std}_\pi\otimes\tau)\otimes\Sym^5_\pi=(\Sym^6_\pi\oplus\Sym^4_\pi)
\otimes\tau$, \texttt{sexticTau\_bank}, and the quintic square
$\Sym^5\otimes\Sym^5=\bigoplus_{j\le5}\Sym^{10-2j}$,
\texttt{quinticSquare\_bank}, ready as pure citation): the twist demand of
each successive converse grows linearly while the product supply is fixed, so
the full conjecture requires an engine not present in current mathematics ---
the Galois-theoretic engine is unavailable for Maass forms, and the
trace-formula program remains unexecuted beyond its first cases.  The open
input of this entire subject is therefore \emph{one object}: a
coefficient-dependent mechanism producing the one-sided twisted reflections
at unbounded rank.  Its minimal test case is the $(r,m)=(5,4)$ reflection.

\begin{remark}[falsifiability]\label{rem:falsify}
The reduction of Theorem~\ref{thm:reduction} is falsifiable at every stage:
a computed violation of the nine-moment convergence, of local positivity
(Proposition~\ref{prop:sumsq}), or of any bank identity of
Proposition~\ref{prop:tower} at any $n$ would refute the corresponding
machine-checked statement.  No violation is known; the identities have been
verified symbolically at every $n$ by compilation.
\end{remark}

\section{What is achieved and what is not}\label{sec:status}

Achieved, unconditionally: the exact zero-slack reduction; the nine-moment
single-form record with its coefficient algebra machine-checked end to end;
exact local Rankin--Selberg positivity; the three new joint-moment entries at
Serre grade (subject to Remark~\ref{rem:check}); the two insufficiency
theorems; and the reduction of the frontier to the two named faces of
Proposition~\ref{prop:faces}.

Not achieved, by anyone: the Sato--Tate conjecture for a single Maass form.
Its remaining content is exactly the object named at the end of
Section~\ref{sec:frontier}, and no assembly of existing results closes it ---
that negative statement is itself proved here at the three levels of
Theorems~\ref{thm:blind} and~\ref{thm:monoid} and the exhaustion facts of
Section~\ref{sec:insufficiency}.
