\begin{abstract}
Rung 9 of the benchmark ladder, stated as compiled theorems rather than a register
claim.  For every self-dual-multiset Satake bank and every admissible archimedean chart
--- pure complex $\prod_i\Gm(s+\mu_i)$ or mixed $\Gr(s+\delta)\prod_i\Gm(s+\mu_i)$ ---
a \emph{single pointwise identity} between the two prescribed theta readouts of the 3D
bank machine-yields the complete analytic package: entire continuation, functional
equation $\Lambda(1-s)=\varepsilon\,\Lambda(s)$, and the chart identification on the
initial half-plane.  Every other input is compiled: local integrability, rapid decay at
both ends, the Mellin identification, the contragredient comparison, and the identity
theorem globalization.  The mixed case is powered by a new compiled clock: the Gaussian
$2x^{\delta}e^{-\pi x^2}$ with Mellin transform exactly Deligne's real factor
$\Gr(s+\delta)$, convolved in signed-log height with the complex-pair kernel.
Instantiating at the symmetric powers of a level-one eigenform seed --- the self-duality
input is the compiled clock reversal --- gives the mechanism at \emph{every rank}
$\Sym^r$ and, at $r=4$, finishes rung 5: the $\Sym^4$ package from one identity at its
mixed chart $\Gr(s+\delta)\,\Gm(s+(k-1))\,\Gm(s+2(k-1))$, weight $k=2n$.
\end{abstract}

\section{The mechanism at a complex chart}

Fix a finite channel type $\iota$ and a polynomial Satake dual pair $W$ on $\iota$: a
primal weight system and its contragredient, with radial Euler coefficients
$c_j=h_j(\text{weights})$ and $c^\vee_j$ (complete homogeneous symmetric functions), and
polynomial growth exponents.  The two prescribed 3D readouts are the theta banks of
these coefficients against the completion kernel of a shift list $\mu s$: the primal
bank $\Theta(x)=\sum_n c_{n}\,\phi_{n}(x)\,g(h_n(x))$ read on the carrier, and the
reciprocal-height contragredient bank.  These are the objects
\texttt{cpsPolynomialFullPrimal3DBankReadout} and
\texttt{cpsPolynomialFullDual3DTransformedReadout} of the corpus; their local
integrability, rapid decay, and initial-half-plane Mellin identification with
$\prod_i\Gm(s+\mu_i)\cdot D(s)$ are compiled facts, not hypotheses.

\begin{theorem}[the mechanism; \texttt{ThetaMechanism.mechanism}; machine-checked]
\label{thm:mech}
Let $W$ be any polynomial Satake dual pair whose contragredient coefficients equal its
primal coefficients ($c^\vee_j=c_j$ for all $j$ --- the self-dual-multiset condition).
Let $\mu s\neq\varnothing$ be any shift list with $\operatorname{Re}\mu\ge 0$, and
$\varepsilon\neq 0$.  Suppose the \emph{one identity}
\[
\Theta\!\left(\tfrac1x\right)
=\varepsilon\,x\cdot\Theta^\vee\!\left(\tfrac1x\right)
\qquad(x>0)
\]
between the prescribed primal readout and the reciprocal-height contragredient readout.
Then the niceness package exists over the shifted bank: an entire $\Lambda$ with
$\Lambda(1-s)=\varepsilon\,\Lambda(s)$ and
$\Lambda(s)=\prod_i\Gm(s+\mu_i)\cdot L(s)$ for
$\operatorname{Re}s>B_W$, where $B_W$ is the uniform threshold
$\#\iota+\max(\text{exponents})+1$ (\texttt{ThetaMechanism.mechB}).
\end{theorem}

The proof consumes nothing beyond the identity: the strong reflection pair assembles
with every analytic field compiled; the contragredient transform equals the primal
transform by the coefficient equality on the open half-plane and the identity theorem
(\texttt{entire\_eq\_of\_eqOn\_isOpen}); the functional equation and chart follow.  The
threshold, the summability of the shifted bank beyond it
(\texttt{ThetaMechanism.bankArithmetic\_summable}), and the half-plane conditions on
the shifts are all uniform in the data --- there is no per-instance analytic input
anywhere.

\section{The real clock and the mixed kernel}

Every completion kernel so far was built from the complex-place clock
$2x^{\mu}e^{-2\pi x}$ with Mellin multiplier $\Gm(s+\mu)$.  The even symmetric powers
require the real place.\\
\texttt{GlobalHelixRealClockCompletion.lean} adds it:

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{gammaRClock\_mellin}; machine-checked]\label{lem:clock}
The Gaussian clock $g_{\mathbb R}^{(\delta)}(x)=2\,x^{\delta}e^{-\pi x^2}$ has
\[
\int_0^\infty g_{\mathbb R}^{(\delta)}(x)\,x^{s}\,\frac{dx}{x}
=\pi^{-(s+\delta)/2}\,\Gamma\!\left(\tfrac{s+\delta}2\right)=\Gr(s+\delta),
\qquad \operatorname{Re}(s+\delta)>0,
\]
exactly Deligne's real factor.  The convergence half-plane is
\texttt{gammaRClock\_mellinConvergent}; the uniform signed-log weighted profile bound
is \texttt{weightedGammaRLogProfile\_bounded}.
\end{lemma}

The proof substitutes $u=x^2$ in the carrier coordinate and lands on the
$\Gamma$-integral; the $2$ in the clock absorbs the Jacobian $\tfrac12$.
The \emph{mixed kernel} is then one real clock convolved in signed-log height with the
complex-pair completion kernel,
\[
\texttt{mixedKernelLog}
=g_{\mathbb R}^{(\delta)}\ast_{\mathrm M}\bigstar_i\,2x^{\mu_i}e^{-2\pi x},
\]
and the three kernel facts that power everything are compiled at every $\delta$ and
every finite list: uniform boundedness at every admissible weight (an $L^1$ profile
convolved with a bounded profile is bounded), decay faster than every power, and the
exact multiplier ---
\[
\texttt{weightedMixedKernelLog\_bounded},\;\;
\texttt{mixedKernelLog\_rapid},\;\;
\texttt{mixedKernelLog\_hasMellin}:
\]
\[
\widetilde{g}(s)=\Gr(s+\delta)\cdot\prod_i\Gm(s+\mu_i).
\]
On this kernel the full 3D bank layer is rebuilt verbatim: the banks
\texttt{mixedPrimal3DBankReadout} and \texttt{mixedDual3DTransformedReadout}, their
theta identifications, local integrability, rapid decay, and the two initial-half-plane
identifications \texttt{mixedPrimal3D\_initialIdentification} and
\texttt{mixedDual3D\_initialIdentification}.

\section{The mechanism at a mixed chart}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{ThetaMechanism.mechanismR}; machine-checked]\label{thm:mechR}
Let $W$ be self-dual-multiset as in Theorem~\ref{thm:mech}, let $\delta$ have
$\operatorname{Re}\delta\ge0$, and let $\mu s\neq\varnothing$ have nonnegative real
parts, $\varepsilon\neq 0$.  One pointwise identity between the mixed primal readout
and the mixed reciprocal-height contragredient readout yields the mixed package
(\texttt{MixedNicePackage}): entire $\Lambda$,
$\Lambda(1-s)=\varepsilon\,\Lambda(s)$, and
\[
\Lambda(s)=\Gr(s+\delta)\cdot\prod_i\Gm(s+\mu_i)\cdot L(s)
\qquad(\operatorname{Re}s>B_W).
\]
The continuation in a mixed package is unique
(\texttt{MixedNicePackage.unique}).
\end{theorem}

Theorems~\ref{thm:mech} and~\ref{thm:mechR} are \emph{one} mechanism at the two
archimedean chart types in classical use.  Neither knows anything about modular forms:
the inputs are a self-dual finite weight multiset, a chart, and one reflection.

\section{Every rank of the eigenform seed; the $\Sym^4$ finish}

For the $\Sym^r$ clock of a level-one weight-$2n$ eigenform seed the self-duality
input is not a hypothesis: the contragredient of the $\Sym^r$ weight system is its own
clock reversal, so $c^\vee_j=c_j$ at \emph{every} rank
(\texttt{Sym3ThetaWall.symrPair\_dual\_eq\_primal}, consumed here), and the shifted
radial coefficients are the $\Sym^r$ bank
(\texttt{symrCoefficientArithmetic\_eq}).  Instantiating:

\begin{corollary}[\texttt{symr\_package\_of\_theta},
\texttt{symr\_mixed\_package\_of\_theta}; machine-checked]
For every rank $r$, every admissible chart of either type, and every
$\varepsilon\neq0$: one theta identity of the prescribed $\Sym^r$ readouts yields the
complete package for $L(\Sym^r\!,s)$ over the compiled $\Sym^r$ bank, at the uniform
threshold.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[rung 5's finish; \texttt{sym4\_package\_of\_theta}; machine-checked]
\label{cor:sym4}
Weight $k=2n$, $n\ge1$.  With the exponent-pair shifts
$\mu_1=k-1$, $\mu_2=2(k-1)$ (\texttt{sym4Shifts}) and any admissible real shift
$\delta$: one pointwise identity of the mixed $\Sym^4$ readouts yields entirety, the
functional equation, and
\[
\Lambda(s)=\Gr(s+\delta)\,\Gm(s+(k-1))\,\Gm(s+2(k-1))\cdot L(\Sym^4\!,s)
\]
on the initial half-plane.
\end{corollary}

The complex shifts are pure representation theory --- the nonzero exponent pairs
$\pm(k-1),\pm2(k-1)$ of $\Sym^4$ of the discrete-series parameter --- and match the
classical completions (for $k=12$: $\Gm(s+11)\,\Gm(s+22)$, the $\Sym^4\Delta$ chart;
compare Shimura's $\Sym^2$ chart $\Gr(s+1)\Gm(s+k-1)$ \cite{Shimura}, whose complex
shift is the $i=1$ case of the same law $\mu_i=i(k-1)$).  The real shift $\delta$ is
carried parametrically: the middle exponent of $\Sym^{2m}$ is $0$ and contributes
$\Gr(s+\delta)$ with $\delta\in\{0,1\}$ fixed by the archimedean parity of the middle
line; the theorem consumes whichever $(\delta,\varepsilon)$ satisfies the identity ---
the identity itself pins the chart, no table is consulted.  This mirrors the corpus's
own register at the identification proposition: one $\Gr(s+\delta)$ adjoined for even
$r$ (\texttt{universal.tex}, Theorem \texttt{prop:localid} --- the global
identification of the candidate with $\Sym^r\pi$).

With this, the ladder state is: rung 4 ($\Sym^3$) finished from one identity at its
pure-complex chart (previous note); rung 5 ($\Sym^4$) finished from one identity at its
mixed chart (Corollary~\ref{cor:sym4}); and rung 9 --- \emph{a new mechanism} ---
stated and proven as the two generic theorems above, of which every per-rank finish is
a two-line instance.

\section{Register}

\begin{remark}[register]
\textbf{Proved (machine-checked).}  Everything named above: the real clock and its
Mellin identification; the mixed kernel's bounds, decay, and multiplier; the mixed 3D
bank layer; the two mechanism theorems; uniqueness; the all-rank and $\Sym^4$
instances.  All at axiom footprint
$\{\texttt{propext},\texttt{Classical.choice},\texttt{Quot.sound}\}$, no
\texttt{sorry}.

\textbf{Consumed from the corpus.}  The compiled theta/Mellin engine (theta banks of
polynomial coefficients, identification, decay); the compiled clock-reversal
self-duality of $\Sym^r$; the compiled seed data of the eigenform
(\texttt{HeckeEigenData}).

\textbf{Cited, not formalized.}  The classical discharge of the one remaining identity
per even rung --- automorphy of $\Sym^4$ (Kim \cite{Kim}, Kim--Shahidi
\cite{KimShahidi}) implies the reflection classically; $\Sym^2$ is Gelbart--Jacquet
\cite{GJ} with Shimura's chart \cite{Shimura}.  These are named for scope, not
consumed.

\textbf{Not claimed.}  The truth of the theta identity for any $r\ge2$ is not claimed
here; it is the isolated input, exactly one pointwise equation per rung.  The value of
$\delta$ for a given even rank is not asserted; the theorem is uniform in it.

\textbf{Falsifier.}  Any self-dual-multiset bank with a chart of nonnegative shifts
satisfying the one identity whose $L$-readout fails entirety, the functional equation,
or the chart identification on the stated half-plane would refute the mechanism; so
would a mixed kernel whose Mellin multiplier deviates from
$\Gr(s+\delta)\prod_i\Gm(s+\mu_i)$ at any admissible point.
\end{remark}
