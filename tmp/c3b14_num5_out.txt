D2 -- TOTAL POSITIVITY, re-adjudicated
==============================================================================
  Block 4 printed 'TP is FALSE' while measuring 0/5 TP2 violations.  The printed
  verdict was unsupported and is WITHDRAWN.  The correct structural statement does
  not need the minors at all:

    TP2 for K(a,b) = R(a-b) is EQUIVALENT to log-concavity of R (classical).
    But log-concavity does NOT imply a nonnegative transform.  Witness, exact:
      W(v) = exp(-v^4) is even, positive, and log-concave ((log W)'' = -12 v^2 <= 0),
      yet exp(-|v|^p) is positive definite ONLY for p <= 2, so its transform takes
      negative values.  Measured below.
      transform of exp(-v^4): min = -1.872725e-01 at xi = 4.600  -> NEGATIVE, as claimed
    So even if R IS log-concave (=TP2), that gives nothing.  And TP_infinity (PF)
    would force, by Schoenberg, Rhat = 1/Psi with Psi in Laguerre-Polya -- a
    statement about Rhat being zero-free, which is not what is wanted and is not
    available.  The determinantal family is dead because it does not IMPLY the
    conclusion, not because R fails its hypotheses.
